The haushaut Visualizer takes the guesswork out of choosing a new facade or roof. You upload
a photo of the house, pick a haushaut product, and see a realistic preview on the real
building within seconds.

It's built for haushaut customers and partner businesses such as roofers and facade
installers, who use it during consultations to show clients the finished result on the spot.

## How it works

1. Sign in with an existing haushaut account
2. Take a photo or choose one from the gallery
3. Select the facade or the roof
4. Apply a haushaut product and see the result straight away

## Features

- Apply products to the facade and the roof separately
- Browse the full haushaut range with its colours and finishes
- Compare several variants on the same photo
- Save previews and share them with clients or family
