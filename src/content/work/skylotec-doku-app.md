Every fall-protection installation has to be documented, and that record is part of the
annual inspection and is handed to the building owner with the invoice. The SKYLOTEC DOKU APP
moves this paper form onto the phone.

Installers record everything the documentation requires:

- Building details
- Installation company
- Name of the responsible installer
- Product designation
- Fasteners used
- Roof plan

With the record in place, every single anchor point can be identified at any time.
